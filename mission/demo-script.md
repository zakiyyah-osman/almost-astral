# Demo script

About 2 minutes. Write what is said and what is clicked at each step.

1. **Hook:** the situation in one or two sentences.
2. **Layers:** switch through the layers and name the mission for each.
3. **Plan:** pick the stops; the route appears and avoids the hazards.
4. **Brief:** distance, time, elevation profile, radiation dose, conditions.
5. **Preview:** click a stop to see the photo of what the crew will find.
6. **Twist:** a solar storm alert; the plan switches to the return route.
7. **Close:** one sentence about the real NASA data behind it all.
