# Demo script

About 2 minutes. DRAFT: lines in *(brackets)* change once the real data is in. Practise it twice, with a stopwatch, before the pitch.

Before starting: open the app in Planning view, layers all off, large text off, nothing selected.

1. **Hook (10 s).** Say: "Two astronauts have one outing on Jezero's ancient river delta. What is safe to walk, what is worth the trip, and when must they turn back?" Show the map.
2. **Layers (25 s).** Switch on terrain, minerals, thermal and the hazard layer one at a time. Name the mission each time: HiRISE and CRISM on MRO, THEMIS on Mars Odyssey. Point at the red cells: "These are no-go areas." *(Say which layers are real and which are still fake.)*
3. **Plan (20 s).** Point at the numbered stops and the dashed route. Say: "Three science stops, and the route stays out of the red." Click a stop: read its name and why it matters. *(Add the photo here if we have one.)*
4. **Brief (15 s).** In the Plan card: total time against the 360 minute allowance, walking distance, radiation dose. Say: "The outing uses under half the allowed time."
5. **HUD (35 s).** Switch to HUD and press Simulate EVA. Show the compass heading, distance to the next stop, time back to the rover, and the oxygen, battery and radiation gauges. Say: "This is what the crew sees in their helmet."
6. **Twist (15 s).** Press Solar storm. The HUD turns red and the crew takes the shorter way back. Say: "The storm data on the HUD comes from NASA's DONKI database."
7. **Close (10 s).** Say: "Every layer names its mission and instrument. Where a number is our estimate, like the radiation dose at Jezero, the app says so."

## If something breaks

- Map does not load: reload once. If still blank, show the recording.
- HUD stuck: press R to reset, then Simulate EVA again.
- Keep a screen recording of the full run on the laptop as the backup.

## Questions to expect

- **Is the data real?** Say which layers are real and which are fake, exactly as shown in the layer badges.
- **Why Jezero?** It has a delta, clays and carbonates in one place. *(Check the facts in `mission/brief.md` first.)*
- **Why is radiation an estimate?** Perseverance carries no dose instrument. Curiosity's RAD measures it at Gale, so we scale from its published numbers.
